## Regulatory interpretation and availability evidence

- Quebec government, [2025-12-17 announcement of rent-setting changes](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/des-janvier-une-nouvelle-methode-de-calcul-plus-simple-et-previsible-pour-fixer-le-cout-des-loyers-67770): known at the forecast cutoff; rules effective 2026-01-01. New method uses a three-year average of Quebec general CPI with adjustments. General CPI is not rent CPI. No property expenses, notice dates or leases' section F are supplied; do not compute a legal allowable increase from these extracts.
- TAL, [2025 annual release](https://www.tal.gouv.qc.ca/sites/default/files/COMMUNIQUE_FIXATION_2025_FR.pdf), published 2025-01-21: explains annual components of calculation, not a universal ceiling and not the 2026 rate. Quebec rental jurisdiction does not prove that ordinary rent-fixing applies to every new building.

Legal applicability remains unresolved without actual leases and readiness dates.

## How public information enters the project
The notebook displays the dated public table, contrasts Quebec and Ontario, and reconciles market easing with a cautious recent-history central forecast. High provincial rent CPI and broader-market rent growth are not substituted for this extract's lower effective-growth median. Ontario's conditional guideline and Quebec's new-building provisions prevent an unsupported hard cap. No numeric coefficient is assigned without comparable historical vintages and sufficient validation. `available_external()` filters publication dates in both final diagnostics and backtests; all curated numeric observations are later than historical cutoffs except Ontario's 2026 announcement, which is still later than those cutoffs. Historical models therefore do not use future public data. Public integration is contextual, not an externally fitted forecasting model.

Full downloadable historical CPI/CMHC panels and building-specific legal documents are not integrated; this is a stated limitation, not an invented feature. The project runs offline after dependency installation because public aggregates are versioned here.
